# Prioritisation: 12 ideas scored with ICE (M2)

Score = Impact x Confidence x Ease, each from 1 to 10. Impact = expected incremental members active at 3 months; Confidence = strength of the evidence today; Ease = time and people needed in 90 days. Numbers in the "evidence" column come from the simulation unless marked public.

Ideas 5 and 6 are not growth actions but measurement debts: they decide the next reallocation. Idea 7 is rejected because the test shows a novelty effect, not a lasting lift.
