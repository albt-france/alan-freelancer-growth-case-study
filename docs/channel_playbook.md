# Channel playbook (M4)

One paragraph per channel. Numbers are **simulated** (104 weeks) unless marked public.

**Brand search.** Converts best per click (7.1%) and looks cheapest (EUR 23 per deduplicated sign-up), but the holdout test X4 shows only 1% of its sign-ups are incremental (at most 20%). Keep a defence budget (about EUR 2,900 a week) on the brand name and monitor competitor bidding weekly; re-test once a year.

**Non-brand search.** Large volume (32,571 sign-ups) and the weakest 3-month activity (72%). Marginal cost today about EUR 188 per incremental sign-up (response curve). Keep near today's level; move bids towards queries tied to setting up a business ("micro-entreprise", "statut freelance") in January and September-October, the public peaks of creations.

**LinkedIn Ads.** The only channel that targets by job title, so it reaches consulting and IT freelancers (42% of its clicks). It reports 1.97x its deduplicated sign-ups and its total conversion rate is flattered by that mix (Simpson's paradox: best in total, worst in every sub-segment). The geo test X3 measures EUR 229 per incremental sign-up versus EUR 44 claimed. Hold spend, retest at x2 for 12 weeks, judge on incremental cost only.

**Partnerships (Partner A, B, C: accounting tools and banks for freelancers).** Best 3-month activity (84%) and the lowest cost per incremental sign-up (EUR 59, with an assumed 80% incremental share until a test proves it). Double the budget in 90 days; negotiate placement at the moment of business creation (onboarding of the accounting tool, account opening). Build a partner-level holdout (one partner paused for 4 weeks) to measure its real incremental share.

**Referral.** Cheap and well retained (80%) once 12 self-referral codes are removed (361 referees, none active). Pay the reward on activation, cap rewarded referrals per code, keep EUR 50 (X2: a larger reward only creates a 3-week novelty spike).

**AI search (visits from AI assistants).** Small and growing (3,043 sign-ups, from about 10 to 45 a week). No paid lever and no measurement today. **Proposed protocol, not run in this study:**
1. A fixed panel of about 50 questions freelancers ask when choosing health cover ("best health insurance for a freelancer in France", "mutuelle pour micro-entrepreneur", in French and English);
2. ask them each month to the main AI assistants, same wording, fresh session;
3. record whether the brand is cited, its position and the sources the answer links to;
4. track **share of citations** (brand cited / questions asked) and the referring traffic tagged in analytics;
5. act on the sources the assistants cite (help pages, comparison pages, partner content) and check the next month's panel.
No AI assistant was queried for this study.
