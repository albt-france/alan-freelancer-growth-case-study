# 90-day plan

Budget numbers come from the simulated response curves (`src/allocate_budget.py`): same total, EUR 46,000 a week.

| | Today | Recommended |
|---|---|---|
| Brand search | 10,000 | 2,900 |
| Non-brand search | 14,000 | 12,600 |
| LinkedIn Ads | 12,000 | 13,600 |
| Partnerships | 6,000 | 12,000 |
| Referral | 4,000 | 5,000 |
| Incremental sign-ups / week (model) | 503 | 557 (+11%) |

19% of the weekly budget moves.

## Days 1-30: reallocate what is proven, start what must be measured
- Week 1-2: cut brand search to the defence budget (reversible in a day).
- Week 1-4: negotiate placements with Partner A/B/C; first partner live in week 5.
- Week 1-3: referral guardrails live (pay on activation, cap per code).
- Week 2 onwards: creative rotation at frequency 4, one sub-segment angle per batch; first monthly AI search question panel.
- Week 2: start the LinkedIn geo retest (x2 spend, 12 weeks, 20 matched pairs).
- Week 3-7: rerun X1 (landing page by sub-segment) with one bot filter before assignment; 61,116 visitors per arm.

## Days 31-60: read and adjust
- Read X1 at week 7 (split check first).
- Partners live: weekly cost per retained member by partner.
- Second AI search panel; act on the sources the assistants cite.

## Days 61-90: decide with the new evidence
- Read the LinkedIn retest (week 13); refit the curves with X1 and LinkedIn results; reallocate.
- Partner-level holdout to measure partnerships' real incremental share.
- Decide on local campaigns in the five lead departements (public market data).

## Every week
One-page review: north-star (incremental active members per euro), guardrails, live tests with the decision rule written in advance, one learning written down.
